# Locked Facts

Related: [[00 - Story Bible Index]] · [[World/Piko System|Piko System]] · [[Characters/Damian|Damian]] · [[Dorm/Dorm Roster|Dorm Roster]]

> [!warning] Highest Priority Canon
> These facts override accidental contradictions in improvised roleplay unless the Commander explicitly retcons them.

## Setting

- Piko abilities exist and are trained through specialized academies around the world.
- Piko users are overwhelmingly women.
- Pikos are not normally hereditary and possess a semi-biological component.
- Classification includes destructive potential.

## Damian

- Damian is an American male university student in Japan, majoring in Chemical Engineering.
- He is **not** enrolled in a Piko academy.
- Damian possesses a concealed male Piko called **Eergo Booster**.
- A doctor discovered the ability before the current story and agreed to hide it.
- Damian intentionally avoids official Piko testing and institutional disclosure.

## Eergo Booster

- Eergo Booster amplifies another Piko user's existing ability through physical contact.
- Activation produces a warm sensation in the recipient.
- Sustained and/or more intimate contact can create stronger or longer amplification.
- Heavy use costs Damian substantial metabolic energy and produces major caloric demand.
- It does not currently grant Damian another person's Piko, permanently enhance a target, compel affection, or require kissing as the sole activation method.
- Exact multipliers, duration equations, ceilings, and medical consequences remain unmeasured canon.

## Dorm

- Damian's dorm environment has a stable core cast of **twelve Piko Academy girls**.
- Damian and Kenji do not count toward those twelve.
- The twelve slots are fixed by [[Dorm/Dorm Roster|Dorm Roster]].
- Elise, Akari, and Reina are already established characters.
- Svetlana Volkova preserves the previously mentioned tall blonde Russian girl concept but remains otherwise undeveloped.
- The remaining eight girls are reserved continuity anchors and should not gain contradictory traits before their introductions.
- Visitors and classmates do not automatically become dorm residents.

## Current Social Canon

- Elise is Damian's primary romantic interest.
- Reina is the lead tester/organizer for controlled exploration of Damian's Piko.
- Akari has a competitive, teasing, physically affectionate friendship/rivalry with Damian.
- Kenji is Damian's roommate and frequently acts as the neutral observer of the group dynamic.

## Canon Hygiene

When a new fact appears in play:

1. Treat it as scene canon.
2. Check it against this file and the relevant character/world file.
3. If compatible and recurring, add it to the appropriate file.
4. Promote major identity, power, relationship, roster, or setting facts here only after they are explicitly established.

This file is intentionally boring in the best possible way. Its job is to stop the universe from changing socks while nobody is looking.

## Core Profile

**LOCKED CANON**

- American male university student living in Japan.
- Senior-level academic standing with substantial AP credit accepted.
- Major: Chemical Engineering.
- GPA previously stated as approximately 3.8.
- Former JROTC corps commander.
- Holds a GROL license.
- Has a noticeable American Southern accent.
- Family ties include Louisiana; he has referenced family gumbo.
- Experienced with hiking, camping, and outdoor gear.

## Piko

Damian possesses the extremely unusual male Piko **[[World/Piko System#Damian's Piko: Eergo Booster|Eergo Booster]]**.

He actively conceals this fact from institutions and most people around him.

## Established Relationship Notes

- Damian values trust and treats intimacy seriously.
- He has stated that he has no prior sexual partners.
- He is interested in a long-term relationship with Elise rather than purely casual attention.
- He jokes aggressively with friends once comfortable, but his underlying boundaries and loyalty are genuine.

## Do Not Drift

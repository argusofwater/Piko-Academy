## Piko

Damian possesses the extremely unusual male Piko **[[World/Piko System#Damian's Piko: Eergo Booster|Eergo Booster]]**.

**Author-level truth:** Eergo Booster is fundamentally an energy storage, transfer, and network-support Piko. Damian acts as a biological battery/hub. He can store compatible energy, boost others, borrow portions of connected allies' reserves to reinforce his own body, relay energy between connected people, and use available energy to accelerate natural healing/recovery.

He **cannot copy or mimic connected Pikos**. The network integrates energy reserves and condition awareness, not powers.

The complete mechanics are deliberately unknown to Damian and the current cast. They must discover them through experience and testing. See the Piko System file for the locked mechanics and knowledge boundary.

Damian actively conceals his male Piko status from institutions and most people around him.

# Damian Connected Circle

Related: [[Characters/Damian|Damian]] · [[Characters/Dorm Girls|Dorm Girls]] · [[World/Piko System|Piko System]]

> [!important] Canon scope
> This file records the women currently established as connected to Damian's developing Eergo network and locks their Piko names/mechanics. **The cast does not understand the complete Eergo network model.** Connection behavior should be discovered naturally in play.
>
> This is a relationship/network roster, not a declaration that every person listed is a dorm resident. Ayame and Yuki have academy roles outside the student dorm roster.

## Elise Laurent

**Piko:** **Arc Step**  
**Role:** Electrical close-range speed / enhancement

Elise generates and channels bioelectricity through her body. She can electrify herself, discharge arcs through contact, and use tightly timed electrical impulses through her muscles and nervous system for explosive acceleration and heightened reflexes.

**Limits:** Heavy use causes muscular fatigue, tremors, electrolyte depletion, and neurological strain. Arc Step is primarily bodily enhancement and close-range electrical discharge, not unlimited long-range lightning projection.

**Damian interaction:** Extra Eergo energy can increase the energy available to Arc Step, but Damian never gains electrical powers from Elise.

## Reina Tachibana

**Piko:** **Vector Glass**  
**Role:** Force redirection / defense / control

Reina creates transparent glasslike planes that redirect kinetic force striking them. Impacts, projectiles, falls, and other moving forces can enter a plane and be redirected along a chosen vector.

**Limits:** Reina redirects existing force rather than generating it. Stronger impacts require stronger constructs, greater concentration, and greater Piko expenditure.

## Akari Fujimoto

**Piko:** **Thermal Dominion**  
**Role:** Thermal transfer / area control

Akari moves thermal energy between targets and areas. She can heat one region while cooling another, establish steep temperature gradients, and weaponize heat or cold by redistributing available thermal energy.

**Limits:** She does not possess an infinite heat source. Large changes require an available thermal reservoir and can destabilize her own body temperature.

## Valentina Orlova

**Piko:** **Vector Bind**  
**Role:** Directional restraint / movement control

Valentina places invisible directional anchors on objects or people she touches. Once marked, she can bias or force the target's movement toward, away from, or around an anchored direction.

**Limits:** She can maintain only a limited number of anchors. Increasing mass and resistance sharply increase strain and energy consumption.

## Misaki

**Piko:** **Afterimage**  
**Role:** Stored movement / precision physical technique

Misaki can record a physical movement she personally performs and later replay that movement once with supernatural precision and speed. Dodges, kicks, leaps, throws, and similar actions can be stored.

**Limits:** She must physically perform the original action before storing it and can hold only a small number of recorded movements at once.

## Hana

**Piko:** **Resonance**  
**Role:** Vibration / sound / structural disruption

Hana manipulates vibration and resonance. She can amplify or dampen sound, transmit vibration through materials, sense movement through surfaces, and identify or exploit resonant frequencies to weaken objects.

**Limits:** Resonance requires a medium. Powerful or poorly controlled use can damage Hana's hearing, equilibrium, and body.

## Mio

**Piko:** **Slipstream**  
**Role:** Airflow / personal mobility

Mio controls airflow in the immediate region around her body. She can reduce drag, generate directed currents, accelerate movement, soften falls, extend jumps, and redirect small airborne objects.

**Limits:** Her effective radius is short. Slipstream is not weather control, and sustained high-speed use exhausts her rapidly.

## Sora

**Piko:** **Fold**  
**Role:** Short-range spatial movement

Sora can temporarily shorten the distance between two places she can simultaneously perceive, allowing her to cross several meters in a single step by folding the intervening space.

**Limits:** She requires line of sight/perception of both endpoints. Longer folds become exponentially harder, and she cannot safely fold through unknown enclosed spaces.

## Kaede

**Piko:** **Bloom**  
**Role:** Organic construction / restraint / defense

Kaede produces branching hardened organic constructs from her body, visually resembling pale wood or coral. She can form shields, restraints, braces, climbing structures, and temporary armor.

**Limits:** Bloom consumes large quantities of water, minerals, calories, and Piko energy. Recursive uncontrolled growth can become self-feeding and catastrophic.

**Cascade continuity:** Kaede has experienced a runaway Cascade involving uncontrolled Piko output/growth. Damian absorbed the runaway surplus energy. He later routed that stored surplus into Elise's recovery. The cast does not yet possess the full battery/network explanation for what happened.

## Ayame Kuroda

**Role:** Vice Dean  
**Piko:** **Golden Chorus**  
**Role:** Synchronization / support / disruption

Ayame emits expanding golden waves of light that synchronize living nervous systems. Cooperative targets can experience improved coordination, balance, reaction timing, and resistance to panic or disorientation. Stronger directed pulses can interfere with a hostile target's motor timing.

**Limits:** Golden Chorus is not mind control and cannot force thoughts, beliefs, loyalty, or decisions. Synchronizing large groups creates substantial neurological strain. Disrupting unwilling targets is much harder than supporting cooperative ones.

**Visual signature:** The established strange golden light-wave phenomenon is Golden Chorus in action.

## Yuki

**Role:** Student Liaison  
**Piko:** **Stillpoint**  
**Role:** Momentum suppression / defense / rescue

Yuki creates localized zones in which momentum is progressively suppressed. Objects and people moving through a Stillpoint field lose speed, allowing her to soften falls, slow projectiles, control movement, and create defensive pockets.

**Limits:** Momentum is suppressed progressively rather than erased instantly. Greater speed and mass consume more of a field's capacity, and Yuki can maintain only a limited number of zones simultaneously.

# Power Separation Guardrails

These Pikos must remain mechanically distinct:

- **Elise / Arc Step:** bioelectricity and electrically driven bodily acceleration.
- **Reina / Vector Glass:** redirects existing kinetic force using planes.
- **Akari / Thermal Dominion:** transfers thermal energy.
- **Valentina / Vector Bind:** imposes directional anchors after touch.
- **Misaki / Afterimage:** stores and replays her own movements.
- **Hana / Resonance:** manipulates vibration and resonant frequencies.
- **Mio / Slipstream:** controls local airflow around herself.
- **Sora / Fold:** shortens perceived spatial distance.
- **Kaede / Bloom:** grows hardened organic constructs.
- **Ayame / Golden Chorus:** synchronizes or disrupts nervous-system timing through golden waves.
- **Yuki / Stillpoint:** progressively suppresses momentum in localized zones.

Damian's Eergo Booster can interact with the energy demands and output of all of these abilities, but **never grants Damian the ability itself**.

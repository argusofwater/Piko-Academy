# Dorm Girls

Related: [[Dorm/Dorm Roster|Dorm Roster]] · [[Characters/Damian|Damian]] · [[World/Piko System|Piko System]]

> [!note] Canon status
> The dorm contains **exactly twelve Piko Academy girls** as its stable core resident cast. Elise, Akari, and Reina are established in play. The remaining names below are continuity anchors only until their personalities, appearances, nationalities, and Pikos are developed on-screen.

## 1. Elise

**Status:** Established canon

- Piko Academy student.
- Analytical and observant.
- Damian's primary romantic interest.
- Comfortable enough with Damian for private affection and teasing.
- Damian ranks her first among the girls currently closest to him.
- Their relationship is developing toward something serious, but should not be treated as formally defined unless established in play.

## 2. Akari

**Status:** Established canon

- Piko Academy student.
- Red-haired.
- Competitive, loud, physical, and extremely comfortable teasing Damian.
- Rival/friend energy with Damian includes wrestling, headlocks, hugs, and transport jokes.
- Damian nicknamed her **The Spicy Pepper**.
- Damian ranks her third among the girls currently closest to him.

## 3. Reina

**Status:** Established canon

- Piko Academy student.
- Quiet, controlled, observant personality.
- Functions as translator/mediator and the group's lead tester for Damian's Piko.
- Organizes notes and spreadsheets concerning testing.
- Damian nicknamed her **Silent but Deadly**.
- Headpats are an established affectionate privilege.
- Damian ranks her second among the girls currently closest to him.

## 4. Svetlana Volkova

**Status:** Reserved continuity

- Piko Academy student and dorm resident.
- Intended to preserve the already-mentioned concept of a tall blonde Russian girl entering Damian's social orbit.
- Specific Piko, personality, academy ranking, and relationship with Damian remain unestablished.

## Roster Rule

Do not spontaneously add a thirteenth resident to the core dorm cast. New recurring Piko Academy girls may be classmates, visitors, friends, rivals, faculty, residents of another floor/building, or replacements after an explicit roster change.

## Continuity Use

A reserved resident is canonically present in the dorm but remains deliberately undefined. A scene may introduce her naturally, after which established details should be moved into [[Characters/Dorm Girls|Dorm Girls]] and, if important enough, [[Continuity/Locked Facts|Locked Facts]].

Visitors do **not** alter the twelve-resident count. If the story permanently changes residency, record the departure/replacement explicitly rather than silently changing the roster.

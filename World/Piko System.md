# Piko System

Related: [[Characters/Damian]] · [[Characters/Dorm Girls]] · [[Characters/Damian Connected Circle]] · [[Continuity/Locked Facts]]

## General Piko Framework

**LOCKED CANON**

- Pikos are magical or supernatural abilities found overwhelmingly in women.
- They are not normally hereditary.
- Pikos have a semi-biological component and can be studied, measured, and classified.
- Specialized academies exist around the world to train Piko users.
- Piko categories are organized at least partly by destructive potential.
- Male Piko users are sufficiently rare that Damian believes discovery would attract institutional or governmental attention.

## Damian's Piko: Eergo Booster

**LOCKED AUTHOR CANON / IN-SETTING MECHANICS PARTIALLY UNKNOWN**

**Name:** Eergo Booster

**Type:** Energy Storage / Transfer / Network Support

### Fundamental Rule

Damian's body functions as a biological battery and distribution hub for compatible biological/Piko energy. He can absorb energy, store a limited quantity inside himself, and redistribute it to himself or people with whom he has formed a connection.

**Damian manipulates energy, not Pikos.** He never copies, mimics, steals, or independently activates another person's Piko. Energy borrowed from Elise does not give him Arc Step; energy borrowed from Ayame does not give him Golden Chorus.

### Boost

- Damian can transfer stored energy into another Piko user's system.
- The recipient's own Piko determines what the extra energy does.
- A boost may increase output, endurance, recovery, efficiency, or some combination depending on the recipient.
- Direct physical contact is the easiest and most efficient transfer method.
- Activation is commonly accompanied by warmth.

### Storage / Battery

- Damian can absorb compatible surplus Piko/biological energy and retain it temporarily.
- His storage capacity is finite.
- Overcharge threatens Damian first, producing escalating fever, tremors, arrhythmia, muscle spasms, neurological strain, and eventually cellular injury.
- Stored energy is fungible as fuel even though the Piko that originally produced or carried it remains unique to its owner.

### Borrow

- Damian can draw a portion of available energy from a connected person.
- With that energy he can reinforce his own body's ordinary physical performance: strength, speed, stamina, recovery, and resistance to exhaustion/pain.
- This is physical reinforcement, not Piko mimicry.
- Eergo Booster naturally resists draining a donor below a safe biological reserve. Damian can deliberately push beyond that safeguard, but doing so endangers the donor.

### Relay

Damian can route energy from one connected person through himself and into another. This makes a mature connection network function as a distributed reserve with Damian as its central transfer node.

All transfers pass through Damian. He is therefore the network's hub and its fuse; serious transfer instability or overload strikes him first.

### Connection Development

The connection is an emergent property of repeated boosting, contact, familiarity, and mutual energetic exposure. The cast does not currently understand its full mechanism.

Observed/developing stages are best understood by the author as:

1. **Contact:** transfer requires physical touch.
2. **Bonded:** Damian can sense broad energetic condition and perform limited transfer at short range.
3. **Integrated:** a persistent network connection exists. Damian can sense the person's energetic presence/condition and route energy through the network without continuous contact.

These are author-facing descriptions, not established scientific terminology known by the cast.

Damian's recent ability to mentally 'pluck' a connection and make connected people aware of his presence is an expression of this network, not telepathy or mind control.

### Healing / Recovery

Damian is not a conventional healer. He can provide concentrated biological/Piko energy that accelerates and supports the body's own repair processes.

- Excellent for exhaustion, bruising, cuts, strains, minor fractures, and other injuries the body can plausibly repair.
- Can potentially stabilize more serious injuries by supplying energy, but does not replace proper medical treatment.
- Cannot create missing matter, remove foreign objects, neutralize arbitrary poisons, resurrect dead tissue, or simply regrow catastrophic losses.
- Healing is energetically expensive.
- Energy used for healing may originate from Damian's stored reserve or, through the network, from another connected donor.

### Kaede Cascade / Elise Healing

**LOCKED EXPLANATION, NOT YET KNOWN BY THE CAST:** During Kaede's runaway Cascade, Damian absorbed the uncontrolled surplus energy rather than destroying or suppressing it. This heavily charged his internal reserve. When Elise was wounded, Damian routed that captured surplus through himself into Elise and used it to drive her recovery.

The energy originated with Kaede, but Damian did not use Kaede's Piko. The sequence was effectively **Kaede → Damian → Elise**.

### Costs and Hard Limits

- **Finite capacity:** Damian can be overcharged.
- **Transfer loss:** energy movement is not perfectly efficient; direct contact is more efficient than remote network transfer.
- **Reserve protection:** the Piko resists dangerous donor depletion unless Damian deliberately overrides the safeguard.
- **Bandwidth:** simultaneous monitoring/transfers become progressively harder and less precise as more people are involved.
- **Hub vulnerability:** all network transfers pass through Damian, so he absorbs metabolic and neurological backlash first.
- **Metabolic demand:** heavy use creates extreme caloric and hydration requirements.
- **No mimicry:** no amount of connection allows Damian to cast another person's Piko.

Exact storage capacity, transfer efficiencies, safe percentages, maximum network range, and mature-network limits remain deliberately unquantified until discovered in play.

## Knowledge Boundary: Preserve the Mystery

**CRITICAL CONTINUITY RULE:** The mechanics above are author-level canon. Damian, Reina, Ayame, academy researchers, and the other connected girls do **not** currently possess this complete explanation.

Characters may infer mechanics from observations, test hypotheses, be wrong, discover pieces gradually, and develop their own terminology. Do not have a character suddenly explain the battery/network model in full merely because it is recorded here.

The story should reveal Eergo Booster experimentally.

## Continuity Guardrails

Do not casually establish any of the following without explicit story development:

- a precise percentage multiplier or storage capacity;
- a fixed duration formula;
- automatic romantic or sexual effects;
- mind control, emotional compulsion, or pheromone effects;
- permanent enhancement;
- Damian stealing, copying, suppressing, or independently using another person's Piko;
- a universal requirement for kissing or sexual contact.

Physical intimacy may improve transfer through sustained close contact, but it is not the source of the Piko and is never mandatory.

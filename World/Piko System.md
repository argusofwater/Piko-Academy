# Piko System

Related: [[Characters/Damian]] · [[Characters/Dorm Girls]] · [[Continuity/Locked Facts]]

## General Piko Framework

**LOCKED CANON**

- Pikos are magical or supernatural abilities found overwhelmingly in women.
- They are not normally hereditary.
- Pikos have a semi-biological component and can be studied, measured, and classified.
- Specialized academies exist around the world to train Piko users.
- Piko categories are organized at least partly by destructive potential.
- Male Piko users are sufficiently rare that Damian believes discovery would attract institutional or governmental attention.

## Damian's Piko: Eergo Booster

**LOCKED CANON**

**Name:** Eergo Booster

**Type:** Support / amplification Piko

**Primary effect:** Damian can temporarily amplify another Piko user's ability through direct physical contact.

### Activation

- Physical contact with a Piko user can trigger amplification.
- The activation is accompanied by a noticeable warm sensation for the recipient.
- More intimate or sustained contact can produce a stronger and/or longer-lasting amplification.
- Accidental contact therefore carries a real risk of unintended activation.
- Damian does not need laboratory equipment or an external device to activate the ability.

### Costs and Limits

- Amplification consumes Damian's own metabolic energy.
- Heavy use produces extreme caloric demand; after substantial use he can require several full meals.
- The ability boosts an existing Piko. It does **not** grant Damian a copy of the target's ability.
- The exact amplification curve, maximum safe output, duration formula, and long-term biological consequences are **not yet canonically measured**.

### Secrecy

- A doctor discovered Damian's Piko before the current story and agreed to conceal it.
- Damian intentionally avoids official testing and disclosure.
- He enrolled in an ordinary Japanese university rather than a Piko academy.
- The secrecy surrounding Eergo Booster is a major ongoing story pressure.

## Continuity Guardrails

Do not casually establish any of the following without explicit story development:

- a precise percentage multiplier;
- a fixed duration formula;
- automatic romantic or sexual effects;
- mind control, emotional compulsion, or pheromone effects;
- permanent enhancement;
- Damian stealing, copying, suppressing, or independently using another person's Piko;
- a universal requirement for kissing or sexual contact.

Physical intimacy may be a **more effective contact method**, but it is not presently established as the only activation method.
